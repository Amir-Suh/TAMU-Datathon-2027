## Objective and frozen controls

Predict the five-category probability vector in payout order $0,1,2,5,20$ at each query's cutoff. Score each row by squared Euclidean error against the simulator probabilities $p_i^*$. Average within an episode across its eight machines, then across episodes in each condition. With $E[c]$ the model error and $B[c]$ the matched frozen reference error,

$$
R=\frac{1}{4}\sum_c\frac{E[c]}{\max(B[c],10^{-9})},\qquad
S_{\mathrm{main}}=\frac{60}{1+R},\qquad
S_{\mathrm{adapt}}=\frac{40}{1+R}.
$$

The four equally weighted conditions are stable, short_history, drift, and shared_change. Use each round's own reference. At the main hybrid's $R$, a reduction of 0.001 in $R$ is about 0.053 main points. [Scoring rules](../RULES.md)

## Evidence behind the queue

- **Win inference.** M3 considers all 28 binary lag pairs with $1\le a<b\le8$, both polarities, and no flip or one flip on a fixed grid. Under a flip hypothesis it reverses the favorable/unfavorable assignment for pre-flip observations, retaining evidence from both phases in the same Beta-Binomial likelihood. M4 adds an episode-shared flip component. Its improvement over M3 is small on released histories (−0.000287 $R$) and larger at 24 older plus 8 recent observations (−0.007853). The repaired M5 win corrector and cutoff-only logistic calibrators degraded their matched controls. [Handoff](../HOUSE_EDGE_AI_HANDOFF.md) · [Mask stress](05_adaptation_stress/README.md) · [Signal calibration](03_signal_calibration/README.md)
- **Payout states.** Main validation diagnostics find two 1×-given-win levels per type, about 0.455/0.754 for steady, 0.214/0.500 for balanced, and 0.121/0.335 for jackpot. Within each type the 2×/5×/20× ratios were nearly constant across states. The high state accounted for about 54% of queries. The high-1× state was associated with about 0.47 times the win odds after controls. The champion's mean win residual was +2.7 percentage points in the high state and −3.2 in the low state. The factored GRU's 1× score had type-level AUC of about 0.86–0.89 for this state on reused main validation. Treat the levels and odds multiplier as diagnostic anchors. Estimate deployable parameters from permitted fitting data. [Payout diagnostics](ceiling_analysis/README.md) · [Payout-state experiment](02_payout_state/README.md)
- **Remaining error.** Averaging the change in loss from correcting each head first and second attributes 48.7% of remaining loss to the win head and 51.3% to conditional size. The inferred (4,7) slice accounts for 45.3% of remaining normalized loss on 32.3% of queries. Short histories account for 35.8% on 25% of queries. Median complete lag-pair support is 36 in short histories and 220 in full histories. A contiguous 40-tick suffix provides 33 complete (4,7) contexts within the suffix. The 1× and 2× classes contribute 55.7% and the 2×/5×/20× tail contributes 34.5%. These overlapping slices describe different partitions of the same loss. [Residual slices](ceiling_analysis/slices.csv) · [Diagnostic definitions](ceiling_analysis/README.md)
- **Label noise.** For sampled outcomes the expected one-hot Brier loss equals squared error to $p_i^*$ plus $1-\sum_k(p_{i,k}^*)^2$. On the 768 released targets, the mean sampling variance is 0.37777. The hybrid's mean squared error to the same probabilities is 0.01236 per query, about 31 times smaller. These are raw query averages. Fit low-dimensional structure on grouped training histories and pseudo-cutoffs. Test official score against released odds. Use seeded, offline known-law fixtures to verify mechanisms. Keep hosted inference such as TypeSafe Jev out of the queue under the offline contract. [Released validation targets](../public/validation/targets.csv) · [Historical evidence](../HOUSE_EDGE_AI_HANDOFF.md) · [Offline contract](../RULES.md)

## Evaluation and promotion

1. **Freeze data and comparisons.** Record round, season, source and input hashes, folds, seed, versions, parent forecasts, and fit scope. Reuse [main episode folds](folds.csv). Freeze matched control arms, variants, target slices, and comparison tier before scoring. Keep each episode and its pseudo-cutoffs in a single fold. Fit every learned component of a strict out-of-fold candidate outside its held episodes, including upstream models, priors, offsets, and combination weights. Use grouped inner folds for selection.
2. **Enforce the cutoff.** Use observations at ticks $u<t$ across all machines to predict tick $t$. Preserve gaps and carry a mask. Use the query's signal only at its recorded cutoff and neutral signal for historical endpoints. Use released training labels for supervised cutoff fits. Condition labels may define splits and evaluation strata. Production routing uses observable support or posterior features. Check prediction invariance after perturbing outcomes at and after the prediction tick.
3. **Label the evidence tier.** Known-law simulations test mechanisms. Previously examined public main validation supports development comparisons. Frozen pair_l31/factor_gru forecasts used released histories across all main splits, so arms inheriting those parents have transductive exposure. Report strict grouped reruns separately. For adaptation, assign a condition-balanced lockbox of approximately one quarter of validation episodes by stable episode hash before viewing its odds. Keep lockbox histories outside learned fits for the strict comparison. Any self-supervision across splits or use of adaptation development odds gets a separate declared arm. Freeze the controls and at most two challengers before opening the lockbox once. [Existing lockbox protocol](FORECASTING_EXPERIMENTS.md#data-and-evaluation-contract)
4. **Use paired episode losses.** Report $R$, points, all four condition ratios, $\Delta R=R_{\mathrm{candidate}}-R_{\mathrm{control}}$, and 10,000 paired, condition-stratified episode bootstrap draws with the same draw applied to model, control, and reference. Report both frozen main controls and the matched ablation control. In adaptation use round-matched forecasts, including carried and adaptation-only controls. Report p0 MSE, win-mass-weighted conditional payout SSE, payout-class contributions, and card-specific slices with counts. Define common slices using frozen pair inference or observed support before comparing. Intervals describe fixed forecasts on the evaluated episodes. [Scorer](score.py)
5. **Promote by tier.** For main, advance only with a paired 95% interval wholly below zero versus the champion, improvement in all four condition ratios, and a matched gain on the card's target slice. Apply the same gate to the assembled forecast. For fresh adaptation, require $\Delta R\le-0.002$ versus the matched incumbent, a paired 95% interval wholly below zero, and no condition deterioration larger than 0.005 unless predeclared. Keep the simpler validated candidate when the evidence is inconclusive. Require clean-room package reproduction before either round is locked.

### E1 · Coupled two-state win and payout filter

- **Fit.** Estimate type-specific low/high 1× probabilities $\mu_{m,s}$ and state prevalence $\pi$ from grouped training histories using a two-component Beta-Binomial mixture. Identify the high state by its larger $\mu$. Combine the positive-count evidence with the causal factored GRU's 1×-given-win score $s_{i,1}^{\mathrm{GRU}}$. The first arm uses the binomial likelihood ratio $L_{\mathrm{high}}/L_{\mathrm{low}}$ for observed counts $n_1,n_{>1}$ at the fitted means, with $L_s=\mu_{m,s}^{n_1}(1-\mu_{m,s})^{n_{>1}}$. Fit the weights on grouped training pseudo-cutoffs and permitted training labels. Missing ticks contribute zero counts.
- **State and win forecast.** Use $\operatorname{logit}\gamma_i=\operatorname{logit}\pi+\beta_{\mathrm{BB}}\log(L_{\mathrm{high}}/L_{\mathrm{low}})+\beta_{\mathrm{GRU}}(\operatorname{logit}s_{i,1}^{\mathrm{GRU}}-\operatorname{logit}\bar\mu_m)$, where $\bar\mu_m$ is the prior-weighted state mean. For Split-Match M3 win forecast $w_i^{\mathrm{base}}$, solve $w_{\mathrm{high}}=\sigma(\operatorname{logit}w_{\mathrm{low}}+\kappa)$ and $(1-\pi)w_{\mathrm{low}}+\pi w_{\mathrm{high}}=w_i^{\mathrm{base}}$. Use the grid $\kappa\in\{-0.85,-0.75,-0.60,-0.45,0\}$ in inner folds. Include endpoint handling for win mass 0 and 1.
- **Joint payout forecast.** Form each state vector $v_s=(1-w_s,\;w_s\mu_{m,s},\;w_s(1-\mu_{m,s})q_i)$, where $q_i$ is a normalized three-element tail. For matched E1 variants use the same tail ratios. First use the base forecast's conditional tail with a train-only type fallback at zero tail mass. Introduce a pooled or shrunk tail separately in E2. Forecast $(1-\gamma_i)v_{\mathrm{low}}+\gamma_iv_{\mathrm{high}}$. The product of *unweighted* state means drops $\operatorname{Cov}(W,R_k\mid\mathcal H)$, with an upward 1× error when high 1× coincides with lower win odds and there is state uncertainty. A win-weighted conditional mix recovers the joint probabilities.
- **Matched arms and readout.** Replay the champion, use a payout-only state mix with $\kappa=0$, apply the win offset with a decoupled state-mean size vector, then average the coupled vectors. Hold the state model and tail constant across matched arms. Compare the static model to a sticky HMM only after the static mechanism passes a known-law check. Use symmetric transition rates 0.002 and 0.005 as the first grid. Report p0 and 1×/2× errors, favorable balanced/jackpot slices, and recovery on known-law coupled fixtures. Z1's sampled-cutoff offset lost to the hybrid, its main-odds fit was post-hoc, and Z2's earlier joint HMM trailed the incumbent. [Z1](research_next/Z1/README.md) · [Z2](research_next/Z2/README.md)

### E2 · Empirical Bayes payout-tail shrinkage

- **Forecast.** Preserve $p_0,p_1$ and tail mass $M=1-p_0-p_1$. From training histories estimate the type tail $q_m$ in class order $2,5,20$. Given the forecast's normalized tail $\tilde r$ and the machine's observed $>1$ count $n$, set $p'_k=M(n\tilde r_k+\alpha q_{m,k})/(n+\alpha)$. Missing ticks contribute zero counts. Define $\alpha=0$ as the identity even at $n=0$, $\alpha=\infty$ as the hard projection, and $M=0$ as an all-zero tail. For $n=0$ and finite positive $\alpha$, use the type tail. Pool across types only if the type's fitting sample has no tail observations.
- **Matched arms and readout.** Compare $\alpha\in\{0,32,64,128,256,512,\infty\}$ on grouped train pseudo-cutoffs. Lock one finite setting before comparing it with the raw hybrid and P0 hard projection. Run the standalone contrast and the matched increment on the retained E1 arm. Verify p0, p1, and total tail mass invariance and report by-type tail error and tail-ratio drift. Select finite shrinkage over P0 only if it earns its added complexity. [P0 replay](research_next/P0/plan.json)

### E3 · Hierarchical four-cell Beta-Binomial M3/M4

- **Forecast-only arms.** Use ordered cells $00,01,10,11$. Start with a pooled mismatch contrast added to the existing match split and keep the M3 hypothesis posterior fixed. Add pair-specific offsets shrunk toward zero with an initial pseudo-count of 150. Fit offsets inside grouped training folds. Hold the champion conditional payout mix fixed. Reuse W1 controls for pooled and pair-specific changes.
- **Full likelihood arm.** For pair, polarity and flip hypothesis $h$, define four canonical emission cells. Put a $\operatorname{Beta}(S_0\mu_c,S_0(1-\mu_c))$ prior on each. Set $\operatorname{logit}\mu_c$ to the type/signal M3 parent logit plus a pooled ordered-cell offset and a shrunk pair offset. To share four rates across a polarity reversal, predeclare a bijection from pre-flip ordered cells to canonical cells that reverses parity. Use XOR on the $t-a$ bit as the first mapping and XOR on the $t-b$ bit as a training-only sensitivity. Keep the raw ordering in the audit output. Multiply the four Beta-function ratios and the matched unavailable-context term to reweight the same pair/polarity/flip grid. Average posterior cell means over hypotheses at cutoff. Keep M4 episode sharing as a separate matched variant. Missing outcomes add no counts. Observed outcomes with incomplete lag context use the unavailable bin in this card.
- **Readout.** Reproduce W1a, W1b, and the W2 comparison before fitting the full likelihood arm. Measure (4,7), high-pair-mass, and overall p0 MSE and $R$, along with cell counts, posterior entropy, and flip/pair recovery on generated histories. Check the frozen-posterior zero-contrast path against its parent and check the likelihood against enumeration for a tiny complete history. [W1](research_next/W1/README.md) · [W2](research_next/W2/README.md)

### E4 · Shift-register gap marginalization and support gate

- **Filter.** Given a fixed hypothesis and transition rates, track the last eight win bits (256 states). From each state, shift in 0 or 1 with its Bernoulli transition probability. At observed ticks keep only successors consistent with the observed bit. At missing ticks retain both successors. Normalize each step and accumulate the log normalizers for the hypothesis evidence. Use a marginal emission where prehistory is unavailable. Propagate the sparse-prefix end state through the contiguous suffix and carry the cutoff state distribution into the win prediction. Identify gapped inputs by observed tick coverage. Parameterize the suffix length to cover the released 40-tick suffix and 16/8-tick stress masks.
- **Likelihood accounting.** The state sum is exact conditional on the specified transition rates and the missing-at-random mask. Use fold-train fixed emissions for the primary filter and the same emissions in its matched drop arm. A local-rate or Beta-Binomial suffix approximation is a separate arm. Carry the boundary state and ensure each observed tick contributes likelihood once. If doing local rate updates, compute each emission before reading that tick's outcome. Measure approximation and pruning effects separately from the exact fixed-rate arm.
- **Support gate.** First compare matched drop versus forward filtering with the Bayesian win head alone, then add a constant blend with a causal tree/GRU win forecast. Test $\lambda_i=\lambda_{\max}\sigma(\eta_0+\eta_1(40-n_{i,\mathrm{eff}})+\eta_2H(\pi_i))$ against that constant. Define $n_{i,\mathrm{eff}}$ as the posterior-weighted count of observed complete lag pairs and $H(\pi_i)$ as the entropy of the normalized 28-pair posterior. Blend the two win masses with $\lambda_i$ and hold the conditional payout head fixed. Fit the gate only in grouped training folds.
- **Readout.** Compare with M3, M4, S2 exact and matched drop, and the S1 constant blend. Score the released short-history slice and deterministic 24+16 and 24+8 masks with the same cutoffs. Report the frozen payout-head comparison for mechanism isolation and an end-to-end rerun with all mask-sensitive parents recomputed. Check short exhaustive paths, full-prefix equivalence under matched emissions, mask invariance, and two-core timing. S2 improved its matched drop arm and trailed the champion. S1's gate had no clear incremental gain over a constant blend. [S2](research_next/S2/README.md) · [S1](research_next/S1/README.md)

### E5 · Adaptation pair discovery and multi-switch BOCPD

- **Discover pairs.** Start from a uniform prior over 28 pairs. Perform two empirical Bayes iterations, averaging posterior responsibilities and applying a 15% uniform mixture floor. Fit on adaptation training histories in the strict arm. Run the proposal's train/validation/test pre-cutoff history fit separately as transductive development. Compare frozen main carry, adaptation-only refit, and warm starts with adaptation weight $\omega\in\{0.5,0.8,1.0\}$ in the pooled cell and payout prior estimates. Rank pairs within each fitting fold.
- **Build features.** Compare the original three engineered pairs with the top six discovered pairs plus one causal best-of-28 summary. Include current match, all-history and last-40 rule accuracy, and corresponding rule predictions. Accumulate accuracy only when both lags and the outcome are observed. Refit the 31-leaf tree under matched feature and history scopes.
- **Allow multiple switches.** Track run length and 56 pair/polarity rules with a changepoint hazard of 1/128 per tick. The growth branch retains its rule and updates segment statistics. The reset branch restarts those statistics, either flipping polarity on the same pair or drawing a different pair from the renormalized discovered prior. Compare swap probability 0 and 0.25. Fix a deterministic pruning budget before validation and log discarded posterior mass. Across missing ticks, advance the clock with unit observation likelihood and freeze sufficient statistics. Compare the primary elapsed-tick hazard with the proposal's no-reset-in-gaps variant. Predict cutoff win mass by advancing the hazard to the prediction tick and averaging predictive emissions under the retained states. Combine with a matched conditional payout head.
- **Readout.** Stage the known-law suite before kit release using rare pairs (1,8), (2,7), and (3,6), double flips at ticks 90 and 170, and late pair swaps with full and sparse histories. Report pair recovery, flip or swap detection delay, raw five-class SSE, runtime, and mask budget. After release compare prior refit alone, dynamic features alone, and single-flip M3/M4 versus multi-switch on adaptation development, then on the frozen shortlist. The prior generated 24+16 carry/refit SSE of 0.212595/0.056175 is a synthetic stress result. [Prior transfer and masks](05_adaptation_stress/README.md) · [Switching controls](research_next/README.md#remaining-experiment-cards)

### E6 · Signal-conditioned neural residual

- **Inputs and loss.** Use a 128-unit factored GRU with an 8-dimensional learned type/signal embedding, observation mask, tick/256, causal structured prequential win logit, and top-pair match evidence. Predict the win logit as the structured offset plus a regularized learned residual. Use a four-logit softmax for positive payout size. Train win loss on observed targets and size loss on observed positive targets. Shift the history input so the model sees ticks strictly before the target. A missing input gets a dedicated token and mask 0. A missing target contributes zero loss. Use the recorded signal on permitted actual training cutoffs and neutral signal at historical pseudo-cutoffs.
- **Matched arms and readout.** Start from a zero residual and compare paired three-seed unaugmented, signal/residual, and signal/residual plus mask-augmentation arms. Mask 25% of eligible training prefixes deterministically to 24 older plus 40 recent ticks. Compare standalone forecasts and hybrid swaps against a common structured baseline and the existing factored GRU. Report p0 MSE, −1 to +1 signal slope, short-history $R$, full-prefix $R$, seed spread, and Torch-to-NumPy and NumPy-to-package parity. In the earlier signal diagnostic, the plain GRU's unadjusted signal gap was 3.68 percentage points against 7.35 points in the released odds. N1 is an earlier smaller residual baseline.
